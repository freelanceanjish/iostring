The Organisation

Zenith Education Network is a K-12 group spanning 48 schools across three Indian states, 28,000 students, 1,200+ teaching staff, and a leadership team that believed deeply in the power of education to change lives.

In 2023, they had a problem. The world had changed. Their classrooms had not.

  • Mixed-grade schools from Grade 1 to Grade 12
  • Urban and semi-urban campuses across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Telangana
  • Established in 1988, a proud legacy and a complex bureaucracy
  • National and ICSE curriculum tracks running in parallel

The Transformation Chronology

Months 1–2 // Discovery: Local operations mapping via on-site classroom data collection parameters.

Months 2–3 // Strategy: Collaborative alignment workshops establishing OKR targets across branches.

Months 3–8 // Launch & Embed: Bootcamps certifying 48 internal Digital Champions across multi-school sprint cycles.

Months 8–12 // Mastery Scale: Organizing cross-school Learning Squads, resulting in a 8,000-household live digital seminar execution.

Months 12–14 // Sustain & Exit: Launch of an autonomous internal Agile Center of Excellence handling operations natively.

The problem

COVID revealed the gap. Digital acceleration widened it.

When remote learning became mandatory in 2020, Zenith scrambled, deploying Zoom, Google Classroom, and a new LMS within weeks. Two years later, the tools were paid for. The adoption was not happening.

By 2023, teacher digital confidence was critically low. LMS usage was at 18%. Student engagement scores had dropped. Meanwhile, Zenith's competitors were pulling ahead with interactive digital content, personalised learning paths, and data-driven teaching.

01

LMS adoption at 18%, teachers uploaded resources but did not use interactive features, gradebooks, or parent communication modules.

02

Teacher resistance was high, 62% of staff in a baseline survey said they felt "unsupported" during previous technology rollouts. Trust in leadership was fragile.

03

Siloed decision-making, IT, academic leadership, HR, and school principals were not talking to each other. Every department had a different tool, vendor, and priority.

04

No transformation metrics, progress was measured by budget spend and training session attendance. Nobody was tracking actual adoption or student impact.

01

Trust is the technology. In education, adoption blockers are almost never technical. They are relational. The moment a teacher trusted their Digital Champion, their LMS usage tripled.

02

The new frontier

Your teams adopted AI.
Nobody redesigned how humans work with it.

In the Netherlands, 95% of organisations have deployed AI tools across teams. Copilot, Jira AI, custom models, digital co-workers. The dashboards look great.

But the Scrum ceremonies were not redesigned. The Definition of Ready still assumes a human wrote every story. Velocity went up. Team trust quietly went down. The humans in the process became validators, not creators, and nobody named that transition.

We coach the layer that AI cannot touch.

AI
AI-ready ceremonies

Redesigning Sprint events for teams where AI generates first drafts, flags risks, and summarises standups. Human judgment stays central.

TC
Human-AI team contracts

Explicit agreements on what AI decides, what humans decide, and how both are reviewed. Clarity that prevents quiet disengagement.

MX
Metrics that still mean something

When AI inflates velocity, DORA metrics need human context. We help teams build dashboards reflecting real delivery health.
